# Extra topics slides

Advanced topics for students who finished the neural-network part. One HTML file per topic, built on the same engine as
[`../machine_learning`](../machine_learning) and [`../neural_networks`](../neural_networks): styles, navigation, logo and
shared figures come from [`../assets`](../assets), and the decks also load `../neural_networks/assets/nn.css` and
`nn-core.js` (cards, code blocks, lab slides, step widgets, `TT()` labels). Open `index.html` to present.

| File | Topic | Lab in the deck |
|---|---|---|
| `01_gnn.html` | Graph neural networks, in four parts. Graphs as data: applications, nodes, edges, features and the adjacency matrix, node/edge/graph tasks, why the node order must not matter (equivariance, invariance). Message passing and the GCN: neighbour sum = $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{X}$, self-loops and normalisation, the GCN layer with numbers on a 5-node toy graph, layers = hops, the computation tree, semi-supervised training (Cora), a GCN on the karate club. Variants and limits: the Laplacian and its modes, GCN as a low-pass filter, over-smoothing, GraphSAGE, GAT with numbers, transformers as GNNs on the complete graph, sum/mean/max, the WL test and what it cannot see. Tasks and practice: readout and batching, link prediction and edge leakage, gather + scatter on `edge_index`, PyTorch Geometric, a practical guide | Extra 1 |
| `02_clustering.html` | Clustering: what a cluster is and why units matter, k-means (Lloyd with numbers, convergence, k-means++, elbow and silhouette, mini-batch, colour quantisation), Gaussian mixtures and EM (monotonicity, k-means as the hard limit, BIC), hierarchical clustering and dendrograms, DBSCAN and HDBSCAN, spectral clustering, internal and external indices, no free lunch, digits, Gower distance | Extra 2 |
| `03_autoencoders.html` | Autoencoders and VAEs: reconstruction losses as likelihoods, PCA as the linear autoencoder, overcomplete/sparse/denoising/convolutional AEs, why an AE cannot generate, KL, the ELBO two ways and with numbers, the Gaussian KL, reparameterisation vs score function, β-VAE, posterior collapse, blurry samples, conditional VAE, VQ-VAE and latent diffusion | Extra 3 |
| `04_gans.html` | GANs: generators as push-forwards, the minimax game with a numeric round, the optimal discriminator and Jensen–Shannon, saturation, mode collapse, two-player dynamics, the Wasserstein GAN and gradient penalty, transposed convolutions and DCGAN, conditional GANs, pix2pix/CycleGAN/StyleGAN, FID, precision/recall, GANs vs VAEs vs diffusion | Extra 4 |
| `05_anomaly_detection.html` | Anomaly detection: kinds of anomaly and base rates, z-score and masking, median/MAD and IQR, Mahalanobis and MCD, k-NN, KDE, GMM, LOF step by step, Isolation Forest, one-class SVM, PCA and autoencoder reconstruction error, ROC-AUC vs PR-AUC, precision@k, thresholds from costs, features, explanations, drift | Extra 5 |
| `06_dimensionality_reduction.html` | Dimensionality reduction: the curse of dimensionality, PCA as maximum variance = minimum error, eigenvectors vs SVD, choosing k, standardisation, eigenfaces, probabilistic PCA, random projections and JL, LDA, LSA, NMF, kernel PCA, classical MDS, Isomap, t-SNE and what it hides, UMAP, kNN preservation | Extra 6 |
| `07_diffusion.html` | Diffusion models: forward noising and its closed form, schedules and SNR, the Gaussian posterior, the ELBO as a noise-prediction MSE, DDPM training and sampling, denoising as score estimation, Langevin, the SDE/ODE view, DDIM, classifier-free guidance, cross-attention, latent diffusion, the U-Net, VAE vs GAN vs diffusion | Extra 7 |
| `08_self_supervised.html` | Self-supervised and contrastive learning: pretext tasks, two views, SimCLR and the InfoNCE loss as a cross-entropy with numbers, temperature, alignment and uniformity, augmentations, the projection head, batch size; collapse, BYOL/SimSiam, Barlow Twins/VICReg; MAE, DINO, CLIP; few-label curves and when SSL pays off | Extra 8 |
| `09_explainability.html` | Explainability on a portfolio with a known truth: interpretable models, impurity vs permutation importance, PDP, ICE, ALE, surrogates, exact Shapley values and their axioms, interventional vs conditional SHAP, KernelSHAP/TreeSHAP, LIME, gradients, Integrated Gradients, Grad-CAM and the sanity check, counterfactuals, fairness by group | Extra 9 |
| `10_conformal.html` | Uncertainty and conformal prediction: aleatoric vs epistemic, prediction vs confidence intervals, heteroscedastic Gaussian networks, the pinball loss, deep ensembles and MC dropout, Bayesian linear regression, calibration and temperature scaling; split conformal step by step, why ⌈(n+1)(1−α)⌉, the coverage guarantee and its Beta law, marginal vs conditional coverage; normalised scores and CQR, prediction sets (LAC, APS), Mondrian; distribution shift, adaptive conformal inference, jackknife+ | Extra 10 |
| `11_causal.html` | Causal inference and uplift: Simpson's paradox on the kidney-stone data, potential outcomes, ATE/ATT/CATE, naive difference = ATT + selection bias, why randomisation works and the four assumptions; DAGs, chain/fork/collider, collider bias, the backdoor criterion, do and bad controls; regression adjustment, matching, propensity scores, IPW, AIPW, bootstrap intervals, refuters, DiD and IV; uplift's four customer types, S/T/X-learners, Qini curves, targeting by risk vs by uplift | Extra 11 |
| `12_reinforcement_learning.html` | Reinforcement learning: the agent–environment loop, how RL differs from supervised learning; bandits (ε-greedy, UCB, Thompson sampling, regret); MDPs, the Bellman expectation and optimality equations, policy and value iteration on a gridworld with numbers, discount and noise; Monte Carlo vs TD, SARSA vs Q-learning on the cliff, DQN; REINFORCE, baselines, actor–critic, PPO; RLHF, reward hacking and the KL penalty, RL in practice | Extra 12 |

Figures: one `assets/fig-<slug>.js` per deck (figure names prefixed by the slug, e.g. `FIG['gnn-…']`, `FIG['cl-…']`) and,
where a deck shows lab results, an `assets/<slug>-data.js` exported from the notebook's own run (each file's header says how).
Lab results quoted in a deck come from that run; some widgets compute their own small toy examples in the browser.

Every deck follows the course pattern: a "where we are" slide, worked examples with real numbers, scripted demos that
advance with → (`data-auto` on a widget), a summary, a vocabulary slide and a green lab slide for its notebook in
[`../../notebooks/extra`](../../notebooks/extra). Editing works exactly as described in
[`../machine_learning/README.md`](../machine_learning/README.md).

The class PDFs (`tools/make-handouts.mjs`) only cover `machine_learning/` and `neural_networks/`: the extra decks are web-only.
